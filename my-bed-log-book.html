Welcome to My Bet Logbook
Our Bet Logbook is the ultimate Module to track your selections and see how it performs. It's designed to display both planned and actual bets, giving you full control over your betting strategy.

Here's what you can expect from our Bet Logbook Module:

If the horse you choose is scratched, it will be removed from the 'planned table' and updated in the table for scratched horses. This allows you to review and make another selection in that race, ensuring that you never miss out on a promo opportunity.
