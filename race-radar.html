Welcome to Race Radar

Horse racing: the golden opportunity for matched bettors in Australia

Race Radar offers convenient filtering options based on race course or race time or even runners count. What sets Race Radar apart is the ability to track multiple upcoming race odds simultaneously, allowing you to place bets quickly when you spot a matched opportunity. For avid gamblers, Race Radar provides almost real-time market fluctuations across all races, enabling informed betting decisions. The Race Radar extension screen is your key to unlocking a world of betting opportunities.

Here's how it works:

  • The screen displays all races for the selected date, with useful filters.
  • Adjust the bookie stake and switch between real money and bonus cash effortlessly.
  • Access Bet View to see odds available for your desired bet amount (defaulted to $25)
