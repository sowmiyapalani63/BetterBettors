Welcome to Race Radar

Horse racing: the golden opportunity for matched bettors in Australiar

Race Radar offers convenient filtering options based on race course or race time or even runners count. For avid gamblers, Race Radar provides almost real-time market fluctuations across all races, enabling informed betting decisions. The Race Radar extension screen is your key to unlocking a world of betting opportunities.
