Welcome to My Bookie Promo

At BetterBettors, we understand the importance of never missing out on a good race promotion. That's why we load general promotions onto your account every single day, all year round!

Here's how it works: Based on your bookie details in the 'My Bookie' section, our system will automatically load all the general promos for the day onto your account. You can expect this to happen around 11 AM AEST.

But that's not all! We offer you the convenience of adding promos for future days, allowing you to plan your bets in advance. Imagine how much more prepared you'll be! Received an SMS or email with exclusive promos from your bookie? No worries! In addition to the system-added promos, you can also add extra promos through the 'My Bookie Promos' section. And to make things even easier for you, we've added a 'Promo' and 'Bookie' filter, so you can quickly find what you're looking for. Never miss a race promotion again with BetterBettors - your ultimate racing companion!
